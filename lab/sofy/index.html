Sofy — rework

Chapter 0 is the original 2018 Android version (copy translated to English). Chapters 1–4 rethink it for iOS 27, before Figma. iPhone 402 × 874, dark like the original, brand mint as the only accent. One story: a person picks what they'd go to, finds a plan for this afternoon, asks the host to let them in, posts their own plan and lets people in, then shows up, checks in and earns coins. People, places and photos come from the original file.

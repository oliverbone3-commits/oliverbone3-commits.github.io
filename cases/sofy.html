Meeting people through plans, in 2018 and today

Sofy was my own app: anyone posts a plan, like coffee at eight or a concert tomorrow, and others nearby ask to join. I built it with the same friends I later made AMO with, and we launched it on Product Hunt in January 2019. Here is the original, and how I would design it today.

Idea, product and design
Team
Me and the friends I later made AMO with
Product
Sofy, an app for meeting people around plans
Timeline
2018–19 · launched January 2019 · rethought in 2026

How do you want to read the case?

Sofy started from a habit of going out together. Anyone posts a plan, the host lets people in, and you see who is going before you ask. The first version was Android for Minsk; we rebuilt it in English for iOS with check-ins and party coins and launched it on Product Hunt in January 2019. We never raised money. In 2026 I redrew it for iOS 27: the start time leads every card, free seats are a row of circles, and the host decides on facts like how often someone actually showed up.

01 · The idea

Something to do tonight, with people you don't know yet

  • Own idea
  • Minsk
  • Plans first

At Ultimate Guitar the team went out together a lot, and the habit stayed with us. Later, living in Minsk, we wanted to build something fun so we wouldn't spend evenings at home. The idea was simple: anyone posts a plan, like coffee at eight, cocktails on a veranda or a concert tomorrow, and others just join.

The idea was mine. I did the product and the design, and built it with the same friends I later made AMO with.

02 · The 2018 version

Interests, a feed of plans, and a host who lets you in

  • Android
  • Match score
  • Host approval
  • Telegram chat

Interests picked at sign-up shaped the feed and a match score with other people. A plan showed who was going, when and how far, and came with a Telegram chat for the group. The host approved each request, so you knew who you were going out with.

We then rebuilt it in English for iOS and added check-ins with a photo from the spot and party coins for showing up, which ranked people on a city leaderboard. The coins were meant to become our own token, since tokens were everywhere in 2018.

03 · How it ended

Launched, and never funded

  • Product Hunt, January 2019
  • No investment

Know who would pay for it before the screens are done.

Note to self

We launched on Product Hunt in January 2019. We didn't raise money, and honestly we didn't know where to look. A few years later apps built on the same idea appeared.

For me it was the first product I owned end to end: the idea, the screens, the launch and the pitch.

04 · Rethinking it today

The same product, redrawn for iOS 27

  • Time first
  • Seats you can count
  • A record, not stars

In 2026 I redrew Sofy to see what I would keep and what I would change. The core stayed: interests, plans by start time, a host who lets people in, check-ins and coins. What changed is how fast you can decide.

The start time leads every card and every pin on the map. Free seats are a row of circles, so you see how full a plan is before you read anything. The plan page shows the host as a person with a record: plans hosted, and how often they actually showed up.

05 · Letting people in

The host decides on facts

  • What the host sees
  • Requests
  • Closes on its own

Before you send a request you see exactly what the host will see about you: your record, what you have in common, plans you have been to together, and an optional line. If the host doesn't answer in time, the request closes on its own, so nobody waits for nothing.

The host gets each request as a card with the person's record, shared interests and their line, and two buttons: Let in or Not this time.

06 · On the day

Show up, check in, do it again

  • Live Activity
  • Photo check-in
  • Coins as points

When the host says yes, a push says you're in, and a Live Activity counts down to the start with the distance. At the meeting point the plan turns into one button: a photo from the spot, and the coins land.

Afterwards everyone's photos make the plan's page, and Again next week repeats it with the same people. Coins stay, but as points for showing up and hosting, not a token.
